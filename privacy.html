Koti and your privacy

Last updated 9 November 2026. This applies to the Koti website and the Koti app.

What Koti keeps

  • Your account: your email address and a password Koti never sees in plain text.
  • What you put in Koti: money, bills, income, savings goals, lists, reminders and settings.
  • Receipt photos and bill documents, only if you switch on keeping them.
  • What Koti remembers about you, the fixes you made to what it read, and how you rated its answers, so it gets things right for you.

It's kept on your device and in your account with Supabase, our database provider. Only you can read it.

Koti's AI

Reading receipts and screenshots, the microphone, talking to Koti and today's note use OpenAI. They're off until you allow them. With them on, what you scan, say or ask, and your own records needed to answer, are sent to OpenAI. OpenAI says it doesn't train its models on this data and keeps it at most 30 days to check for abuse. You can switch the AI off at any time in You → Privacy.

What Koti doesn't do

  • No cookies, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Your data isn't sold or shared with anyone else.

Deleting your account

In Koti, go to You → Delete account. Your account and everything in it, receipts included, are deleted for good.
