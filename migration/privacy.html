What Koti keeps

  • Your account: your email address and a password Koti never sees in plain text.
  • What you put in Koti: money, bills, income, savings goals, lists, reminders and settings.
  • Receipt photos and bill documents, only if you switch on keeping them.
  • What Koti remembers about you, the fixes you made to what it read, and how you rated its answers, so it gets things right for you.
  • If you turn on reminders, the address your phone gives for notifications.

It's kept on your device and in your account. Only you can read it.

Why, and on what basis

  • To run Koti for you: your account and everything you put in it. This is needed to provide the service you signed up for. [NEEDS LEGAL REVIEW: contract, Art. 6(1)(b)]
  • Koti's AI, only when you allow it. You can withdraw that at any time in You → Privacy. [NEEDS LEGAL REVIEW: consent, Art. 6(1)(a)]

Who else handles it

  • Supabase stores your account and records, in the EU (Paris).
  • OpenAI, only with the AI on: reading receipts and screenshots, the microphone, talking to Koti and today's note. What you scan, say or ask, and the records needed to answer, are sent to OpenAI in the United States. OpenAI says it doesn't train its models on this data and keeps it at most 30 days to check for abuse.   • GitHub hosts the website, and like any web host sees the address your device connects from. [Replace with the new host if the website moves elsewhere.]
  • Notifications go through your phone's own service (Apple, Google or your browser's).

Your data isn't sold, and isn't shared with anyone else.

What Koti doesn't do

  • No cookies, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

How long it's kept

For as long as you have an account. When you delete it, everything in it is deleted for good, receipts included. Our database provider's backups are overwritten within [the Supabase plan's backup retention, e.g. 7 days].

Your rights

You can see, correct and download your data in Koti (You → Download a backup), and delete it (You → Delete account). You can also ask us for a copy, a correction or deletion, or object to how it's used, at the address above. If you think we've got it wrong, you can complain to the Data Protection Ombudsman in Finland (tietosuoja.fi) or to the authority where you live. [NEEDS LEGAL REVIEW]
